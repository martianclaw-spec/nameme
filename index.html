The baby name game for couples

NameMe helps expecting parents agree on a baby name — without the endless lists and vetoed suggestions. Each of you swipes through more than 2,600 popular boy names and girl names on your own phone. When you both heart the same name, it's a match. Then face your matches off in a tournament bracket and crown the one.

Browse baby names your way

Swipe today's most popular baby names, timeless biblical names, classic favorites from the 1950s through the 2000s, names starting with any letter, or a random mix — all ranked by real US popularity.

Questions, answered

How does it work?

Start a game, invite your partner by text or email, and swipe separately. Matches are names you both love; the bracket picks the winner.

Is it free?

Completely. No signup, no download — it runs right in your browser.

Boy and girl names?

Both — about 1,600 boy names and 1,100 girl names, ranked by popularity.

Is our game private?

Yes. Your game lives behind a private room code only you two have.
